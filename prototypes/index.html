Five directions for the Els Llibres noticeboard

Working prototypes with the real notices and dates from i4llibres.cat, in Catalan only. Each one takes its look from something families already recognise. Filters and expandable details work; calendar links are placeholders.

  1. 1

    El suro

    The corridor cork board: coloured paper notes held by drawing pins, headlines in the stick-letter handwriting I4 children learn, index cards for the upcoming dates.

    Type: Patrick Hand and Atkinson Hyperlegible. Risk: the tilt and pins carry the whole idea, so everything else stays plain.
  2. 2

    La Mar Bella

    The school's own website, tidied: Poppins and Lato, the school blue in full-width bands, the wave divider, the circular M badge, flat outline icons and capitalised buttons.

    Type: Poppins and Lato, as on lamarbella.cat. Risk: it reads as official, which may be a feature or a problem.
  3. 3

    L’agenda

    The school diary: ruled paper with a red margin, everything in one chronological column grouped by week, and the category filters as the coloured index tabs on the page edge.

    Type: Zilla Slab and Karla. Risk: chronology first means undated notices need a label like "cada setmana".
  4. 4

    La circular

    The photocopied letter that comes home in the backpack: one A4 column, "Benvolgudes famílies", a red date stamp, numbered points, a typewritten box of dates, and a tear-off slip for calendar actions.

    Type: Alegreya and Courier Prime. Risk: the quietest of the five, and the most readable.
  5. 5

    La prestatgeria

    Revised after review: dates box first, one column of notices with a thin colour spine, colour dots in the filter tabs, and a sun and moon theme toggle. The bookshelf is gone.

    Type: Baloo 2 and Figtree. Status: the working direction.

Every page works at 320px and 390px, keeps 44px tap targets, uses native details elements for expanding, and shows visible keyboard focus. Only the bookshelf includes a dark theme; the paper-based directions commit to a single look on purpose.
