Now that it is getting colder, Sandra asks you to **check the spare clothes** your child keeps at school and make sure they are **complete and suitable for the season**:

- trousers
- T-shirt
- underwear
- socks

On rainy days puddles form in the playground, and children can get wet while they play. They may also need a change if they have a little accident. Some children do not have enough clothes to change into, and a complete change keeps them comfortable and dry for the rest of the day.

She asked in her **“Roba de recanvi”** email of **19 October**.
