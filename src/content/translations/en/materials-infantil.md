title: "Materials for the early years play areas"
description: "The early years team is looking for materials to improve the play areas. If you have any at home that you no longer use, you can donate them to the school."
note: "See the requested materials"
---
Contributions are **voluntary**. The early years team has shared this list:

**Camping and play kitchen**

- Small camping chairs and a table, sun shades and large fruit crates.
- A play kitchen and non-plastic kitchen utensils.
- A hammock, lanterns, torches and other camping items.

**Dolls and library**

- Clothes, a cot, games and accessories for dolls.
- Storybooks for the classroom library.

**Workshops and construction**

- Wooden strips of different sizes, no longer than one metre.
- Sturdy cardboard tubes and CDs.
- Small boxes, such as children’s shoe boxes, and fabric.
