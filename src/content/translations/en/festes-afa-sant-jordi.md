note: "What each year does and how to help"
---
The AFA has sent an infographic showing which festival each year group organises in 2026–27, in the email “INFORMACIÓ FESTES AFA ESCOLA LA MAR BELLA”, of 16 November. The festivals committee supports each celebration with a guide and an activity sheet.

**Sant Jordi, on 16 April: I3 and I4.** I4 families organise Sant Jordi again, this time as a team with I3 families, so they can pass on what they learned last year. Some ideas from the infographic: a play, a version of the legend, puppets, storytelling, workshops and games.

**Carnival, on 5 February: years 3 and 4, with every year group.** Each class needs **at least one representative** to go to the preparation and set-up meetings, from December and January onwards. Before Christmas, a committee will be formed with each class’s representatives. The costumes will be chosen in a vote open to every family.

**The other festivals:**

- **Tió**, in November: I5 families prepare a small present for every child in Infantil, I4 included.
- **End-of-year party**, Friday 18 June in the afternoon: organised by year 5 families.

To help the festivals committee, write to [festes.lamarbellaampa@gmail.com](mailto:festes.lamarbellaampa@gmail.com).
