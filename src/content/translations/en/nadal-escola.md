The school has sent its Christmas programme in the email **“NADAL A L'ESCOLA LA MAR BELLA”**, of **27 November**. The infographic is below; here is what concerns the Llibres.

**What to do**

- **From Monday 30 November to Thursday 10 December:** you can bring **products for the Christmas hampers** to **Inma, the caretaker**.
- **Tuesday 15 December, Jumper’s Day:** the children go to school in a **Christmas jumper**, like the whole school community. The programme also puts the **food collection** for the Sagrat Cor parish on that day: the boxes are at the school entrance.
- **Friday 8 January**, the first day of class after the holidays: the children can bring a **toy from the Three Kings** to share with their classmates.

**The Christmas programme**

- **Friday 11 December:** keep an eye on the trees around the school in case **eyes** appear. If you see some, tell the school straight away: it will need to get in touch with Oncle Buscall.
- **Monday 14 December:** **Oncle Buscall** arrives. He looks after the tions, the Christmas logs, all year round, and with his crew, Colgafocs, l’Heura and la Bastona, he visits the schools handing them out. There are **craft workshops mixing year groups**.
- **Wednesday 16 December:** more **craft workshops mixing year groups**.
- **Thursday 17 December:** the infant and first-cycle children get a visit from the **Patge Reial**, the Three Kings’ royal page.
- **Friday 18 December:** **Christmas concerts**. The infant classes sing at **9:30 in Plaça de Sant Bernat Calvó**. The same day, the AFA and Biosca hold the [charity hot chocolate](#avis-voluntaris-nadal-afa) for La Marató. The infographic puts it in the square, and the AFA’s poster under the sports hall porch.
- **Monday 21 December:** the infant and first-cycle children **make the Tió poop presents**, after feeding it in the days before. It is a **shorter school day**; see the [childcare notice](#avis-acollida-desembre).

**The sixth-grade hampers**

As every year, the school is **raffling two Christmas hampers**. The sixth-grade pupils sell tickets at the **afternoon pick-up, at 16:30**, from 27 November. What they raise goes to the farewell celebration they will hold at the end of the year.

The **draws are on Monday 14 and Tuesday 15 December**. If nobody has come forward with a winning number by 16:30 on Friday 18 December, the prize passes to the next closest numbers after it, by the ONCE lottery’s combination, whose holders come to the school by **16:30 on 21 December**.
