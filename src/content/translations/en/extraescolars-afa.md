Families can request enrolment in or cancellation of extracurricular activities organised by the AFA, the family association, **from the 1st to the 20th of every month**. The deadline is **23:55 on the 20th**, and the request takes effect the following month.

To cancel an activity for January, submit the request **by 23:55 on 20 December**. The calendar reminder marks that final day.

The AFA’s rules **do not accept cancellations for December or June**, except on medical grounds backed by a certificate. After a cancellation, a child cannot re-enrol in the same activity for two months. The AFA repeated this on 20 November in the email “Recordatori de la normativa de les activitats extraescolars”.

To take part in the activities, **all payments must be up to date**.

Submit requests through [AFA La Mar Bella’s Ampasoft platform](https://ampalamarbella.ampasoft.net/):

- **Enrolment:** sign in and open “Preinscripció”. Select the child and activity, then click “Realitzar preinscripció”.
- **Cancellation:** open “Preinscripció”, go to the activities tab, select the activity and click “Demanar baixa”.

See the [AFA’s activity rules](https://afalamarbella.cat/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Normativa_Extraescolars_AFA.pdf), in Catalan, and [information about all extracurricular activities on the AFA website](https://afalamarbella.cat/extraescolars/).
