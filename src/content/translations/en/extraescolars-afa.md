Families can request enrolment in or cancellation of extracurricular activities organised by the AFA, the family association, **from the 1st to the 20th of every month**. The deadline is **23:55 on the 20th**.

To take part in the activities, **all payments must be up to date**.

Submit requests through [AFA La Mar Bella’s Ampasoft platform](https://ampalamarbella.ampasoft.net/):

- **Enrolment:** sign in and open “Preinscripció”. Select the child and activity, then click “Realitzar preinscripció”.
- **Cancellation:** open “Preinscripció”, go to the activities tab, select the activity and click “Demanar baixa”.

See [information about all extracurricular activities on the AFA website](https://afalamarbella.cat/extraescolars/).
