As part of the **Escoles Més Sostenibles** (more sustainable schools) project, the **Punt Verd Mòbil**, the city’s mobile recycling point, will come to school on **Monday 9 November 2026**.

That day children can bring things from home to recycle, such as:

- clothes and shoes
- books
- batteries
- light bulbs
- electrical appliances and cables
- cartridges
- oils, paint, aerosols and cosmetics
- tyres

The school’s **“Punt Verd Mòbil Escola la Mar Bella”** email of **29 October** does not say when or where to drop them off.
