The school’s PE department invites children **from I3 to 6è, the last year of primary**, to run the **23rd Cros de Sant Martí**, the district’s cross-country race, on **Sunday 8 November 2026** at the CEM La Mar Bella track (Avinguda Litoral 86-96). It is organised by the Sant Martí district, the Canaletes Sant Martí athletics club and the Barcelona school sports council (CEEB). Taking part is voluntary.

**Sign your child up by 23:59 on Tuesday 3 November** with the [school’s sign-up form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/5qQavBawbi). It asks for each child’s full name, date of birth and health card (CatSalut) or DNI number.

**The Llibres’ race.** Children born in 2022 and 2023 run together:

- Meet at **10:55**; the race starts at **11:10**.
- One lap of the small (green) circuit, 400 m.
- It is a starter race: there are no prizes or rankings, and families can run it together.

When you arrive, go to the CEEB tent to collect the chip and race number. After the race, hand the chip back at the finish. The CEM car park will be closed to the public that day.

Brothers and sisters in other years run at other times, between 10:00 and 12:35. The full timetable is in the sheet attached to the **“ACTIVITAT EDUCACIÓ FÍSICA DIUMENGE 8 DE NOVEMBRE. ESCOLA LA MAR BELLA”** email of **30 October**.
