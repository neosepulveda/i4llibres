You can make a **small creation using autumn fruits and natural materials** with the children: chestnuts, pine cones, leaves, walnuts, acorns, pumpkins, twigs…

Taking part is **entirely voluntary**.

The deadline to bring the creations to school is **Tuesday 27 October**. They will all be displayed for the children to enjoy.
