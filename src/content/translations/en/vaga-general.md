A **general strike has been called for Wednesday 11 November**. From the management team’s email of 6 November:

- The school **will provide the minimum services** that have been set.
- Because the strike is general, it can affect every service, and teachers who are not striking may have trouble getting to school. So **the school cannot promise a normal day**.
- **Menjadors Biosca will let families know** what lunch service it will offer.

The school’s email is “VAGA GENERAL DIMECRES 11 DE NOVEMBRE. ESCOLA LA MAR BELLA”.
