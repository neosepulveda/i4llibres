This is October’s lunchtime programme, the same for every group. Each week has a theme. In brackets is the activity’s play box, where the programme marks one; each box is explained at the end. The original picture is in Catalan.

**1 and 2 October: World Smile Day**

- Thursday 1: group assembly.
- Friday 2: World Smile Day (creativity and emotions).

**5 to 9 October: decorating the dining hall**

- Monday 5: decorating the dining hall (creativity).
- Tuesday 6: relay races.
- Wednesday 7: “peste alta”, a chasing game.
- Thursday 8: petanque.
- Friday 9: musical game.

**12 to 16 October: World Food Day and World Bread Day**

- Monday 12: public holiday, no school.
- Tuesday 13: circle games (emotions).
- Wednesday 14: weighing the food that gets thrown away (ecology).
- Thursday 15: “Res per aquí, res per allà”, meaning nothing here, nothing there (ecology).
- Friday 16: World Food Day and World Bread Day (ecology).

**19 to 23 October: Cooks’ Day**

- Monday 19: storytelling.
- Tuesday 20: Cooks’ Day (creativity and emotions).
- Wednesday 21: rope games.
- Thursday 22: board games.
- Friday 23: musical game.

**26 to 30 October: Castanyada**

- Monday 26: getting ready for the Castanyada (creativity).
- Tuesday 27: decorating the dining hall for the Castanyada (creativity).
- Wednesday 28: October birthdays.
- Thursday 29: Castanyada, the chestnut festival (sport and movement, and emotions).
- Friday 30: musical game.

**Biosca’s four play boxes**

- Emotions: group assemblies, time to listen, mediation and getting along.
- Sport and movement: cooperative games, sport and movement activities.
- Creativity: art, theatre, dance, music and crafts.
- Ecology: sustainability, recycling, reuse and food waste.
