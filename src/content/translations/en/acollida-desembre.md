---
title: "Childcare on 21 December: Biosca will manage the service"
description: "On the shorter school day of 21 December 2026, Biosca will manage childcare from 15:30 to 16:30 and proposes to charge for it. The AFA will no longer provide this service."
note: "What changes for childcare?"
---
On **Monday 21 December 2026**, a shorter school day, **Biosca will manage childcare from 15:30 to 16:30**, taking over from the AFA.

The AFA announced this in its **“JORNADA INTENSIVA 21/12/26”** email of **15 October**. Biosca, which already employs the school’s supervision team, will manage this time slot.

The announcement **does not give prices or booking instructions**.

On **20 November**, in its **“HORA ACOLLIDA DIA 21/12 JORNADA INTENSIVA”** email, the AFA clarified that **the proposal to charge for this childcare comes from Biosca**, not the AFA. It says it does not agree with this approach and has passed families’ complaints on to those responsible. It does not give a price either.
