title: "Childcare on 21 December: Biosca will manage the service"
description: "On the shorter school day of 21 December 2026, Biosca will manage childcare from 15:30 to 16:30. The AFA will no longer provide this service."
note: "What changes for childcare?"
---
On **Monday 21 December 2026**, a shorter school day, **Biosca will manage childcare from 15:30 to 16:30**, taking over from the AFA.

The AFA announced this in its **“JORNADA INTENSIVA 21/12/26”** email of **15 October**. Biosca, which already employs the school’s supervision team, will manage this time slot.

The announcement **does not give prices or booking instructions**.
