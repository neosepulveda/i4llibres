The families of **second-year primary** pupils, this year’s organising committee, are putting on the **Castanyada party**, the autumn chestnut festival, for the whole school on **Friday 30 October, from 17:30 to 19:00**, at Escola La Mar Bella.

There will be **games** for the whole family, creative **workshops**, a **bar** for an afternoon snack and the **Tunnel of Terror**, for anyone brave enough.

**The Tunnel of Terror has only 200 places**, so the organising families recommend buying tickets during the presale:

- **Presale:** 20, 21, 22, 23 and 26 October, 16:30 to 17:30.
- **Bring cash** to buy tickets.

**Bar prices:**

- Water or juice: €1
- Soft drink: €1.50
- Food (hot dog, pizza or sandwich): €2.50
- Crisps or popcorn: €1
- Tunnel of Terror: €1

The AFA sent this in its **“FESTA CASTANYADA 2026”** email of **20 October**.
