The AFA is holding its **first general assembly of members for 2026–27** on **Wednesday 28 October at 17:30**, in person in the **school dining hall**.

**Childcare during the assembly.** If you are going, you can leave your children with the childcare service, at **€2.50 per hour per child**. Sign up on the [childcare form](https://forms.gle/KCcGDmMLKsBDcvwy5) **by 22:00 on Sunday 25 October**. The AFA has extended the deadline, which was 21 October.

**Agenda:**

- Approval of the previous minutes.
- Biosca: a survey on how satisfied families were with the school meals meeting.
- AFFAC: joining its call for solutions for school outings and residential trips, by vote or a statement of support.
- Joining the “Punt d’Ajuda Escolar” project, by vote or a statement of support.
- Blood donation campaign.
- Budget to June 2026 and the accounts: charging for childcare, supervision and the casalet, self-funded parties, what to do with the surplus, and the membership fee or an alternative.
- Fitting out the basement: soundproofing it for piano and drums, and shelves to keep it tidy.
- Damage to the sports hall, claimed from the district and the Consorci.
- Committees: outings, parties, school meals and lunchtime, and those that have lapsed (cakes, grants, safe route to school and the tool workshop).
- Assigned secondary schools.
- Extracurricular activities: rules, organisation and feedback on welcoming newly arrived families.
- Open floor.

The minutes of the last assembly of 2025–26 and the AFFAC proposal are attached to the **“Convocatòria de primera assemblea AFA La Mar Bella Curs 26-27”** email of **22 October**.
