**Charity hot chocolate.** On **Friday 18 December at 16:30**, under the **sports hall porch**, the AFA and Biosca will sell **hot chocolate and melindros** (sponge fingers). It’s for charity: **everything raised goes to La Marató**, Catalan television’s fundraising telethon. They need **volunteers to sell**.

**Oncle Buscall.** The AFA is also looking for volunteers to be **Oncle Buscall**, the character who brings the Tió, at other schools nearby. The volunteers bring the Tió and sing and dance with the children, always in costume so nobody recognises them; the costumes are at each school. If we take part, those schools’ Oncle Buscall will come to ours too. The dates:

- **Escola Pere IV:** Thursday 10 and Friday 11 December, at 15:15 or 15:30.
- **Escola Les Acàcies:** Friday 11 December at 11:30.
- **Escola Sant Martí:** Thursday 10 December at 15:15.

[Oncle Buscall’s website](https://onclebuscall.wordpress.com/) has a video about who he is, and his song, in Catalan.

**How do you sign up?** The AFA’s emails, “XOCOLATADA SOLIDÀRIA 18 DE DESEMBRE” and “BUSQUEM VOLUNTARIES O VOLUNTARIS PER FER D'ONCLE BUSCALL”, of 16 November, don’t say. You can reply to them.
