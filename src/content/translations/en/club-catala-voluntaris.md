---
title: "The AFA is looking for volunteers for the Club de Català"
description: "The AFA’s Catalan conversation club is about to return, but it needs volunteers to chat in Catalan two afternoons a month. Two hours a month is enough to help."
note: "How to sign up"
---
The AFA’s **Club de Català**, “Un cafè i català”, is coming back to Escola La Mar Bella soon, but before it can start it **needs more volunteers**. Plenty of participants are keen to learn and **speak Catalan two afternoons a month**, and few volunteers are available.

If you have **two hours a month** and want to help keep Catalan alive, join the volunteers’ WhatsApp group and say which times would suit you. It is a very rewarding experience.

**Where is the group link?** We don’t publish it here because this page is public. You will find it in the class families’ WhatsApp group, in the message with the “Et busquem!” poster forwarded on 6 October. If you can’t see it, search the group for “Club de Català” or “chat.whatsapp”.
